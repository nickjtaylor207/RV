     disabled, so you do not yet know whether the result survives spread --

     exactly 7 is a result about 7. Rebuild BOTH roller and exiter each pass:
     both carry _last and log across runs.

     spread, and rd=1 is a free daily vega controller with no hedge sleeve.
  4. allow_deltas. A menu with as many columns as constraints makes the solve
     square: unique answer, no cost minimisation. Widen it and the LP starts
     choosing again.
  5. WINDOW. Re-run on sub-windows and compare pnl_per_unit_greek, never raw
     P&L -- different configs carry different risk and raw P&L has no common
     denominator.
